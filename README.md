# VOTARIES (Codename CULTS)
Votaries is a occult-themed, resource-management board game made in godot where you must manage gaining and sacrificing followers, and also be a bit lucky!

The turn loop is as follows:
- The player rolls a dice (or more, depending on the rule set), the number the dice gives is simply referred to as pips.
- The player uses up pips to play cards that they have. Cards are not discarded, and can be used as many times as their pip cost allows.
- Once the player is done using cards, or is forced too by lack of pips, they have the option to sacrifice 5 followers of a specific type to gain a new card. The cards are seperated into 3 groups, Push, Pull, and Prosperity. The player can choose which group to pull from.
- The turn goes to the player counter-clockwise.
